## Title

Electricity production increase from renewable sources and Co2 emission decreases.

## Main Question
<!-- Think about one main question you want to answer based on the data. -->
1.Does electricity production from renewable energy sources decreases the Co2 emission?  

## Description
The project aims to analyze the impact of renewable energy production on CO₂ emissions in the electricity sector. With global concerns about climate change, renewable energy sources like solar, wind, and hydro are being promoted as sustainable alternatives to fossil fuels. This study will investigate whether increased electricity generation from renewables leads to significant reductions in CO₂ emissions and assess the factors influencing this relationship.
