# Project Plan

## Title 
<!-- Give your project a short title. -->
Analysis the Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs) that are currently registered through the Washington State Department of Licensing (DOL)

## Main Question
<!-- Think about one main question you want to answer based on the data. -->
1.What are the key factors influencing the adoption of BEVs and PHEVs in Washington State?
2.How does the geographical distribution of BEVs and PHEVs registrations vary across Washington, and what factors contribute to this?
3.What are the trends in electric vehicle type, make, and model over time?

## Description
This project aims to analyze the adoption and distribution patterns of Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs) across Washington State. By examining factors such as vehicle type, geographical location, economic characteristics (e.g., MSRP and electric range), and CAFV eligibility, the project seeks to uncover insights into the factors driving electric vehicle adoption. Additionally, it aims to identify potential barriers to growth and suggest recommendations for policy adjustments or infrastructure improvements to accelerate the transition to electric mobility in Washington.
